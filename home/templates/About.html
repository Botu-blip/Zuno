About Our Project

Your personal assistant for weekly meals, outfits, and weekend planning.

Eat smart 🍲 | Dress sharp 👕 | Plan better 📅

Who We Are

This platform is designed to help people who struggle daily with small but stressful decisions like what to cook, what to wear, and how to plan weekends.

We combine AI-based smart suggestions with weekly planning to make your life easier and more organized.

Our Key Features

🍲 Smart Meal Planner

Enter your weekend vegetables and get a complete weekly meal plan. Save time and reduce food waste.

Generate Meal Plan

👕 Outfit Matcher

Upload pictures of your top and bottom clothing and get stylish daily outfit suggestions. Don’t like it? Change it instantly.

Try Outfit Suggestion

🎉 Weekend Planner

Not sure what to do on weekends? We suggest fun activities, self-care ideas, and productive plans based on your mood.

Explore Weekend Plans

How It Works

Simply type the vegetables you have at home and we will suggest the best weekly meal plan.

Upload pictures of your top and bottom clothing. Our system will match the best combinations.

You will receive a complete weekly meal plan + outfit plan. If you don’t like it, click "Change Suggestion".

🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Your uploaded images and inputs are used only for generating suggestions. We do not share your data with anyone.
